● 100% NATURAL & HOMEMADE

No chemicals, no additives — just pure spices

Rooted in Nature. Grown With Purpose.

At Elora Spices, every spice and masala is 100% natural and homemade. Sourced directly from the estates and forests of Tamil Nadu and Kerala, our products carry the authentic character of South India's rich agricultural heritage. No chemicals, no shortcuts — just pure, honest ingredients.

15+ YEARS OF NATURAL FARMING

Natural Cultivation

We farm in harmony with natural ecosystems — working with the land rather than against it. Our cultivation practices respect the rhythms of nature, resulting in products with genuine character and integrity.

Respect for the Soil

Healthy soil produces healthy crops. We support agricultural practices that nurture soil biodiversity, maintain natural fertility, and build long-term resilience — ensuring every harvest is as good as the last.

Farmer First

Behind every product is a farming family. We build long-term, meaningful relationships with our growers — ensuring fair value, knowledge exchange, and a shared commitment to naturally inspired farming.

OUR PROCESS

From Farm to Your Home

Every step of our journey is intentional. Transparent. Rooted in respect for the land and the people who work it.

  1. 01

    Cultivate

    Seeds selected for their natural character, grown in soil nurtured through responsible agricultural practices.

  2. 02

    Harvest

    Hand-picked at peak ripeness by experienced farmers who know exactly when each crop is ready.

  3. 03

    Select

    Every batch is carefully sorted and inspected — only products meeting our quality standards move forward.

  4. 04

    Pack Fresh

    Packed at source to lock in aroma, freshness and natural character — minimal processing, maximum integrity.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    From our farms to your home — carefully dispatched to ensure you receive every product at its finest.

OUR STORY

Two Founders. One Purpose.

Elora Spices was born from a shared conviction — that the gap between farm and home should be shorter, more transparent, and more meaningful.

Jeevan

CO-FOUNDER Brand & Business Development

With a deep personal interest in agriculture and natural products, Jeevan drives the brand vision, business development and customer transparency at Elora Spices. His conviction that consumers deserve to know exactly where their food comes from is the founding principle of the brand.

Dr Balaji

CO-FOUNDER Quality & Responsible Practices

An anaesthesia doctor by profession, Balaji brings a healthcare perspective to food quality and responsible production. His belief in the deep connection between soil health, farming practices and human wellbeing shapes every quality decision at Elora Spices.

"We don't just want to sell agricultural products. We want to be part of a movement that brings farming closer to nature."

— Jeevan & Dr Balaji, Co-Founders, Elora Spices

OUR PRODUCTS

Complete Range of Natural Spices & Masalas

From aromatic cardamom and bold black pepper to rich coffee and delicate cinnamon — every product is sourced from the estates and forests of Tamil Nadu & Kerala, 100% natural, homemade, and freshly packed at source.

Coffee

Rich. Bold. Rooted.

Coffee

A rich brew from carefully selected beans, grown in estate conditions that respect the natural growing environment. Elora Spices sources coffee with attention to bean quality, aroma and freshness — bringing you a cup rooted in responsible cultivation.

Origin: South Indian Estates

Green Tea

Delicate. Fresh. Pure.

Green Tea

Plucked at the right stage to preserve its delicate character, green tea represents the careful balance between nature and craft. Our green tea is selected for its freshness, natural colour and quality — a gentle brew that reflects the farm it came from.

Origin: South Indian Tea Gardens

Cardamom

The Queen of Spices.

Cardamom

The Queen of Spices. Grown in the misty hills of South India, cardamom pods are harvested by hand at peak ripeness. Elora Spices selects cardamom for its intense aroma, natural green colour and quality — bringing warmth and fragrance to your kitchen and beverages.

Origin: South Indian Hills

Broken Black Tea

Full-bodied. Brisk. Everyday.

Broken Black Tea

Full-bodied and brisk, broken black tea is the everyday brew rooted in generations of tea estate tradition. Carefully selected for its strength, colour and natural character, this is tea that connects you directly to the estate and the hands that harvested it.

Origin: South Indian Tea Estates

Orthodox Black Tea

Premium. Slow-crafted. Aromatic.

Orthodox Black Tea

Whole-leaf, carefully processed orthodox black tea — a premium, slow-crafted brew that preserves the natural complexity of the leaf. Selected for its aroma, liquor quality and natural character, this represents the art of traditional tea production.

Origin: South Indian Tea Estates

Cloves

Intense. Warm. Aromatic.

Cloves

Harvested before they bloom, cloves carry an intense, warm aroma that has defined spice culture for centuries. At Elora Spices, we select cloves with attention to their natural oil content, aroma and appearance — a spice that brings depth to both cooking and wellness.

Origin: South India

Nutmeg

Aromatic. Warm. Complex.

Nutmeg

The richly aromatic seed of the nutmeg tree, carefully harvested and dried to preserve its natural character. Nutmeg adds warmth, depth and complexity to culinary creations. Elora Spices sources nutmeg with a focus on quality, aroma and responsible cultivation.

Origin: South India

Black Pepper

The King of Spices. Bold by Nature.

Black Pepper

The King of Spices. Bold by Nature. Black pepper vines produce clusters of berries carefully harvested and processed to preserve their natural heat and aroma. Selected from trusted farming communities, our pepper represents the farmer, the soil and the journey from cultivation to your kitchen.

Origin: South India

Turmeric

Golden. Powerful. Purposeful.

Turmeric

Golden in colour, powerful in purpose. Turmeric is one of the most valued agricultural products from Indian farming tradition. Elora Spices selects turmeric for its natural colour, curcumin character and freshness — a root that connects food, farming and wellness.

Origin: Tamil Nadu & Kerala

Cinnamon

Delicate. Sweet. Aromatic.

Cinnamon

True cinnamon — delicate, sweet and aromatic — harvested from the inner bark of the cinnamon tree. Carefully selected for its natural curl, freshness and aroma, our cinnamon brings warmth and character to beverages, baking and culinary traditions across cultures.

Origin: South India

Mace (Jadhikai)

Rare. Complex. Prized.

Mace (Jadhikai)

The lacy red covering of the nutmeg seed, mace is one of the rarest and most prized spices in Indian agriculture. Delicate in appearance but complex in aroma, mace is carefully dried and selected by Elora Spices for its quality, colour and natural character.

Origin: South India
